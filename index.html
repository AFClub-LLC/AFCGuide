Know the platform like you built it.

This guide walks through every screen a parent, a coach, or an admin will ever touch. After reading it, anyone on the team should be able to answer any question a parent or coach asks. Use the search box on the left, or print the whole thing.

Updated July 9, 2026: Foundations weekly enrollment, Elite session packs & closed groups, the Assessment Pathway video, discount codes, the new rewards ladder, progress report cards, the Kaizen feedback board, and the redesigned account page.

What this platform is #

One website, three surfaces. Everything runs on the same database, so a booking made anywhere shows up everywhere.

  • The public site + parent portal: families browse programs, book sessions, load AFC Tokens, and manage their athletes at afsoccer.club (currently the afcdemo Netlify site).
  • The admin Control Center at /admin: dashboards, bookings, clients, payments, coach pay, emails, and site copy. What each person sees depends on their permissions.
  • The coach Session Check-In at /admin/check-in: a phone-first screen where a coach taps who showed up after a session. That tap drives coach pay, loyalty rewards, and referral payouts.

Part 1The parent side

Creating an account & verifying email #

Parents can create an account three ways: from the Sign up page, inline while booking, or after a guest booking. All of them end in the same place: a family account that can book, pay with tokens, and track athletes.

From the Sign up page

  1. Click Get Started (or go to /signup).
  2. Contact: enter full name, phone, and email, then click continue.
  3. Verify: a 6-digit code is emailed. Type it in. The resend button unlocks after 60 seconds if it did not arrive.
  4. Password: choose a password, at least 8 characters. The account is created and the parent lands on a welcome page, already signed in.

The other two doors

  • While booking: on the booking form's Details step, a new parent is offered "Create your AFC account" with a password field. They can also press "Just booking once? Continue as guest".
  • After a guest booking: the confirmation page shows "Create your AFC account". One password and the booking, contact details, and optionally the athlete are saved to the new account.

Accounts created during booking skip the 6-digit code. Instead they get a welcome email with a confirm-your-email link, and the account page shows a banner with a resend button until they click it. Verification is not required to book or pay: it just proves the inbox works so future emails land.

A parent says the code never arrived. What do I tell them?

Wait 60 seconds and press resend, and check spam. On the demo site, see the sandbox note in Known limits: emails only reach the test inbox until the sending domain is verified.

Do parents need to verify email before booking?

No. Booking, paying, and tokens all work unverified. The banner on their account page nags gently until they confirm.

Can one account manage several kids?

Yes. A family account holds any number of athlete profiles, one wallet, and one rewards page.

Logging in & password reset #

Parents sign in at /login (the Log in link in the site header). A login lasts 30 days on that device before it asks again.

Forgot password

  1. Click Forgot password on the login page.
  2. Enter the account email. A 6-digit code is sent (60-second resend, same as signup).
  3. Type the code, then choose a new password (8+ characters). They are signed in right away.

A parent is locked out and email is not working. Can we reset it for them?

There is no admin "set password" button. The reset flow needs their inbox. On the demo, email is sandboxed, so use the seeded demo parent instead of real signups.

Does changing a password log out other devices?

Yes. A password change or reset invalidates older logins everywhere.

Is the admin login the same page?

No. Staff sign in at /admin/login. Same idea, separate door. See Dashboard.

Booking a session (the wizard) #

The Book a session flow is five steps: Service → Time → Coach → Details → Review. A progress bar on top lets parents jump back to any step they already finished.

  1. Service: pick the program. Cards show price, length, and a "What's included" panel. Elite formats carry an "Elite · by acceptance" badge: they can only be booked for an athlete who has been accepted into Elite. Picking a Foundations block here hands off to the dedicated Foundations enrollment wizard automatically.
  2. Time: pick a day on the calendar, then a time. Only days and times with real coach availability show up. Parents can book up to 6 months ahead.
  3. Coach: only coaches who are actually free at that exact time appear. If just one coach is available, this step is skipped automatically.
  4. Details: who is training (signed-in parents tap a saved athlete or "Someone else"), athlete age, parent contact info, optional notes, and the required waiver. New parents see the create-account offer here, with a guest option.
  5. Review: a summary with Edit links per row. "Continue to payment" places the booking and opens checkout.

Paying, and what happens after

  • Checkout offers every payment path the family has: AFC Tokens (whenever the balance covers it), an Elite pack session (for accepted Elite athletes with credits left, see Elite session packs), a reward voucher (a free session or camp discount earned from the rewards ladder), a typed discount code (see Discount codes), or card.
  • One discount per booking: a code, a voucher, or a referral discount, never stacked.
  • Paid bookings become CONFIRMED right away and the family gets a confirmation email plus receipt. An unpaid booking sits as a PENDING request until staff confirm or collect.
  • The confirmation page (and email) includes an add-to-calendar link for Apple and Google Calendar.

Guest booking

No account is needed to book. A guest fills the same five steps, pays, and gets the same emails. Their confirmation page then offers to turn the booking into an account with one password. Until they do, staff will see the booking under Clients with contact info but no login.

Why can't a parent see a coach they wanted?

The Coach step only lists coaches free at the chosen time who also offer that service. Ask them to try another time, or book it for them from admin.

Why is a class missing from the list for their athlete?

Elite formats need an accepted athlete. Performance classes check age band and level: see Performance classes.

The time they wanted disappeared at the last step.

Someone else took the slot while they were filling the form. The wizard sends them back to the Time step to pick another. Nothing was charged.

Do guests really get no account?

Correct, until they set a password on the confirmation page or sign up later with the same email. The guest-account invitation email nudges them too.

AFC Tokens: the wallet #

One family balance for everything. 1 token = 1 dollar, always. No bonus tokens on bigger loads, no expiry, works on any program. Wallet lives at Account → AFC Tokens.

The rules parents ask about

LoadingQuick amounts $100 / $250 / $500 / $1,000, or custom from $20 up to $10,000. Always 1 token per dollar.
SpendingAt checkout, "Pay N tokens" shows whenever the balance covers the price. Tokens confirm the session instantly.
ExpiryNever. That is a selling point over the old session packs.
CancellingA parent can cancel any upcoming session from their account until its start time. If it was paid with tokens, the tokens go straight back to the wallet automatically, with a refund line in their history.
The 24-hour policyTerms and the confirmation email say: give at least 24 hours notice to cancel or reschedule. A late cancel or no-show may forfeit the session or its tokens.
Elite pricesPaying an Elite session with tokens costs the drop-in rate in tokens. The discounted per-session rates now live in Elite session packs, bought on this same wallet page, not in a wallet-load tier.
How the 24-hour policy really works today

The system does not enforce the forfeit automatically yet. The online Cancel button works until the session starts and always refunds tokens in full. If a family cancels inside 24 hours and you decide the policy applies, handle it manually: cancel (or let them cancel), then remove the session's tokens with an adjustment in AFC Tokens (admin), and tell the family why. For a no-show, do not cancel: an admin can mark it cancelled after start, or the coach simply logs the athlete as absent at check-in.

Do bigger loads earn bonus tokens?

No, never. The old load-bonus ladder is gone, and so is the old "load more to unlock a cheaper Elite tier" rule. A token is always worth exactly a dollar; Elite families who want a cheaper per-session rate buy a session pack instead.

Can tokens be refunded to a card?

Not from the site. Cancellations refund to the wallet. Anything beyond that (cash-out requests) is a manual owner decision outside the platform.

Can a family go negative?

No. If the balance does not cover a price, the token option simply does not appear, and the page suggests loading or paying by card.

Where do parents see their history?

The wallet page lists every load, spend, refund, and reward line with dates and notes.

The enrollment wizard, step by step

The wizard lives at /book/foundations: Athlete → Class → Schedule → Review. Picking a Foundations block anywhere else (including the normal booking wizard) lands here automatically.

  1. Athlete: signed-in parents tap a saved athlete (age comes from the birth date); everyone else types a name and age. Outside 2.5 to 12, the wizard gently points the family to Performance instead.
  2. Class: only classes with a slot for that age show up, each with its length, price, locked time, and start date.
  3. Schedule: the whole block is already filled in, week by week, with live seat counts. Nothing to pick unless they want to change a week.
  4. Review: contact details, the create-account offer (or guest), and the liability waiver. "Continue to payment" creates the enrollment and opens checkout.
The same-week swap rule

Can't make one week? That single week can switch to another class in the same calendar week at the same clock time (for example Wednesday Soccer → Friday Soccer, both 5:40). The age group and block price never change, and full classes are shown but not selectable. The server re-checks every swap, so nobody can sneak into a different time.

Paying for a block

  • One checkout pays the whole block, and every week confirms at once.
  • AFC Tokens pay 1:1 whenever the balance covers the block (signed-in families only, since guests have no wallet).
  • A discount code ("Have a discount code?") or a referral discount can come off first; one discount per block, never stacked.
  • Card works too (demo mode for now, like everywhere else).

Accounts and emails

  • The Review step offers inline account creation (one password, optionally saving the athlete profile). Guests can still enroll and claim the account later from the confirmation page; claiming moves the entire block, not just one session.
  • Foundations emails are signed by Mike Monary with his phone and email, not Andre: the confirmation, the receipt, and the welcome email all carry the Foundations contact automatically.
  • A family that creates the account while enrolling gets one combined welcome email (welcome + confirm-your-email button + block details + receipt) instead of three separate ones.

A parent asks when the next block starts.

Today the wizard starts every block at the next upcoming class day: enroll on a Thursday for a Wednesday class and week 1 is the coming Wednesday. Fixed term start dates (real cohort calendars) are waiting on Mike; until then, do not promise a specific "term".

Their athlete is 11 or 12: which time?

They join the oldest band on that day (7-12 or 9-12). If Mike adds a dedicated older band later, the grid update changes this automatically.

Can a family change a week after paying?

The self-serve swap lives in the wizard, before payment. After payment, treat it like any other change: find that week's booking in admin and reschedule it. See Bookings.

A class shows full. Now what?

Each class holds 10 per week. The wizard blocks full weeks and offers the same-week alternative if one exists. Otherwise: another day, or the next block.

Where do staff see who's enrolled?

The Foundations Roster: grouped per class with ages, parent contact, allergies, and per-class CSV export.

The Elite journey #

Elite is by acceptance, not by purchase. The road in is the $199 Assessment Pathway. The reward for commitment is a cheaper per-session rate, and that discount now lives in session packs, not in wallet-load tiers.

The journey, step by step

  1. Family applies at /elite/apply (every Elite button on the site points there). The form asks for the athlete, position, level, goals, days that work, and now a format: Private, 2-player, 3-player, or a 4-6 player group. Picking a group format opens a "Who else is in your group?" roster, so a whole crew can apply behind one $199 assessment.
  2. The application lands in New leads, the business gets a notification email, and the family gets a branded auto-reply with next steps. Applying never grants entry by itself.
  3. The athlete books and completes the $199 Assessment Pathway. Status on their profile moves Applied → Assessment booked → Assessment complete.
  4. Staff make the call on the athlete's profile: Accepted, Waitlist, or Not Yet Fit (routed to Performance instead, with pride intact).
  5. On acceptance, tick "Convert" and the $199 becomes 199 tokens in the family wallet, once per athlete ever, and the family gets the elite-accepted email automatically.
  6. Accepted athletes can book Elite formats, buy session packs, and start closed groups.

Andre's walkthrough video

The Elite page now embeds Andre's Assessment Pathway walkthrough, right under the journey steps. It is click-to-play with closed captions on by default, and the gear menu holds caption size, color, background, and playback speed. If a parent asks what the assessment actually involves, this video is the answer.

A parent asks: is the $199 assessment refundable?

It converts to 199 tokens if the athlete is accepted, so accepted families lose nothing. For waitlist or not-yet-fit outcomes there is no automatic conversion or refund: that is an owner decision case by case.

Does acceptance email the family automatically?

Yes, when you convert. Saving Accepted with the "Convert the $199 to 199 tokens" box ticked sends the elite-accepted email. Saving Accepted without converting sends nothing, so in that case call or email the family yourself.

Where do the public prices live?

The Elite page's rates section shows per-athlete "from / as low as" numbers per format. The exact pack math lives on the family's wallet page and in the admin rate ladder.

How families use them

  • Where to buy: Account → AFC Tokens. The "Elite session packs" section appears for any family on the Elite track and unlocks for purchase once an athlete is Accepted. Pick the format, pick the pack size, pay by card (demo).
  • Spending: at checkout for a matching Elite format, the pack appears as "You have N sessions left" with a Use 1 pack session button. It always drains the emptiest matching pack first, and a pack only ever pays for the format it was bought for.
  • Cancelling: cancel with 24+ hours notice and the session goes back into the pack. A late cancel forfeits it (staff cancellations always refund, any time).
  • Running out: nothing breaks. The next booking simply offers drop-in tokens or card, and the wallet page sells the next pack.

Do packs expire?

No. Sessions sit in the pack until used. That, plus the returned-on-cancel rule, is the honest pitch.

Can a family use a Private pack for a group session?

No. A pack matches its own format only. Different format, different pack (or tokens/card at the drop-in rate).

Can staff see or adjust a family's packs?

See, yes: the admin New booking form shows a live "packs" chip for the family, and staff can spend a pack session on their behalf when booking. There is no admin button to grant or top up pack sessions: fixing a pack mistake means a token adjustment or a comped booking instead.

Why do the Private totals look familiar?

$420 / $760 / $1,020 were the old wallet-load thresholds. Same dollars, completely different mechanic: they now buy a concrete bundle of sessions instead of unlocking a tier.

Elite closed groups #

A closed group is a weekly slot reserved for a fixed set of 3 to 6 families: same coach, same time, nobody else can book into it. A parent with an accepted Elite athlete creates it and invites the others by email.

Anchor parent createsformat, coach, weekly time, size 3-6
→
Invites go outeach family gets an email link
→
Families confirmsign in, pick their athlete, in
→
Slot held weekly4 weeks ahead, each family pays their own seat

How it works

  1. The parent opens Account → Group sessions and hits Start a group (the button appears once an athlete is Accepted).
  2. They pick their athlete, the group format, a coach who offers it, a weekly day and time from real availability, the group size (3 to 6, capped by the format), and the invitee emails.
  3. Each invited family gets an email link. They sign in with the invited email, pick which of their athletes joins, and confirm (or decline, which frees the seat). Confirming is their door into Elite: invited families do not need their own $199 assessment.
  4. Every member's athlete is enrolled into the next 4 weeks of the slot, held as reserved bookings. The time disappears from the public calendar entirely.
  5. Each family then pays for their own athlete's seat per session: pack credit, tokens, or card, at that format's per-athlete rate. Nobody fronts the whole group's bill.

A parent says their invite link doesn't work.

The link only accepts a login with the exact email that was invited. Signed in with a different address, it will refuse: have them sign in (or sign up) with the invited email, or ask the group owner to re-invite the right one.

Can staff create or dissolve a group?

Not today. Groups are parent-created; admin sees their bookings on the calendar (tagged to the group) and the membership on the family's profile, read-only. To end one, ask the owner, or cancel the held bookings from admin.

How big can a group be?

3 to 6 families, but never more than the format's capacity. With today's catalog the biggest group-ready format is the 3-player small group; a true 4-6 group needs that service added to the catalog first.

What if a member never confirms?

Their seat stays "invited" and their athlete is not enrolled. The rest of the group runs normally; the owner can re-send or replace them.

Can a family use tokens for classes?

Yes. Classes cost 40 tokens at checkout like any other session. Elite rate tiers do not change class prices: those are Elite-only.

Can a voucher stack with a discount code?

Progress report cards (Elite) #

A written report card from the coach: skill ratings plus what's going well, what's being worked on, and one thing to practice at home. Elite only: the page and the dashboard tile appear once a family has an accepted Elite athlete.

What's in a card

  • 8 skills rated 1 to 5: first touch & control, passing & vision, dribbling & 1v1, finishing, defending, speed & athleticism, game IQ & decisions, attitude & effort. Any skill can be left unrated.
  • Four short notes: Wins, Working on, At-home focus, and a coach note. All optional, but a card needs at least one rating or note.
  • Cards are on demand, dated documents: write one after a milestone month, an assessment, or whenever there is something to say. There is no forced monthly cadence.

Who writes them: a coach or admin, on the athlete's profile under Clients ("New report card"). Saving is immediately live on the family's account under Progress reports. No email goes out: the report is an in-portal document, so mention it to the family at pickup or in your own message.

Can a Foundations or Performance family get report cards?

Not today. Both the writing form and the parent page require an accepted Elite athlete. Pitch it as an Elite membership benefit.

Can a coach write one for any athlete?

Owners and all-scope staff can. A coach login can write for families they have actually coached, which keeps reports grounded in sessions that happened.

Wrote the wrong thing?

Cards can be edited or deleted from the same place on the client profile. Edits are live to the parent immediately.

Feedback: the Kaizen loop #

One always-open channel for ideas and concerns, feeding one shared board the team reviews together. The parent side is anonymous by construction: the submission stores no name or account, ever.

  • Parents: the "How can we be better?" card on their account page opens a single text box. Submit, done. The confirmation promises anonymity, and the system delivers it: nothing identifying is stored.
  • Coaches and the team add ideas straight on the admin board (attributed by default, with an anonymity toggle in board settings).
  • Everything lands in the same admin Feedback board, where managers triage: New → Planned → Implemented (or Declined), with an optional decision note.

A parent asks if their feedback is really anonymous.

Yes, truthfully. The record stores only the text and "parent" as the source. Nobody, including the owner, can see who wrote it.

Does submitting trigger any email?

No. The loop is deliberately quiet: the board is the single place to review it, ideally on a weekly rhythm.

Recurring weekly slots #

A family can hold the same weekly time with the same coach, month after month. Staff set it up; the parent just decides each month whether to keep it.

How it works

  1. Staff create the series from the admin New booking form by ticking "Repeat weekly: lock this slot for the month". Every remaining week that month books at once, tokens auto-deduct, and the family gets one confirmation email listing all dates.
  2. On the parent's account page a Recurring slots card appears: service, weekday and time, coach, and how far it is booked.
  3. In the third week of each month (the 15th onward), the system opens a 48-hour rebook window and emails the parent: "Keep {athlete}'s weekly slot? 48 hours to rebook."
  4. The parent clicks Keep my slot and next month books automatically, tokens deducted at their usual rate. Or they click Let it go.
  5. No answer within 48 hours means the series ends quietly and the time goes back on the public calendar.

What if the wallet cannot cover the whole next month?

The dates still book so the slot is not lost, but they are marked unpaid, and staff follow up to collect. Nothing silently drops.

What if one week clashes with something already booked?

That week is skipped; the rest of the month books normally.

Can a parent start a recurring slot themselves?

Not yet. It is created by staff, usually after a conversation. Parents manage it from their account once it exists.

When exactly does the reminder email go out?

The daily automation runs at 7:00 in the morning Pacific. From the 15th, any series booked only through the current month gets its 48-hour window opened that morning. See the two daily automations.

Session Check-In (the coach flow) #

Built phone-first. After a session ends, the coach taps who actually showed up, sees their pay compute live, and confirms. That one tap drives coach pay, completion status, loyalty, and referrals.

The flow

  1. The coach signs in at /admin/login on their phone and lands on Session Check-In. They only ever see their own sessions.
  2. Sessions from today and the last 7 days are listed. A session can be checked in once it has finished ("Starts at 3:00 PM, check in once it's done").
  3. Tap the session, then tap each athlete who attended. The list starts empty on purpose: attendance is a positive action, never an assumption.
  4. The button shows the result live: "Confirm · 2 attended · $45". Tap it. Done.
  5. Made a mistake? Fix attendance stays available for 30 minutes (the correction window), then the log locks.

The special cases

  • Nobody showed up: the button becomes "Nobody showed up" and asks for a second tap. The session logs as flagged, $0. An admin reviews flagged sessions in Coach Pay and decides what, if anything, gets paid.
  • Shadow time (paid standby): on-site between sessions but not coaching? The coach logs it here: pick the day, set hours in quarter-hour steps, and it pays at their flat shadow rate (Lucas: $25/hr). Deletable within the correction window.
  • What attendance does to the booking: attended athletes' bookings flip to Completed (which counts loyalty and referrals). Un-attended ones stay as they were, so staff can follow up.
Why attendance matters

Coach pay follows real attendance, not the booking sheet. Three booked, two showed: the coach is paid the 2-athlete rate. The rate snapshot is taken at check-in time, so later rate changes never rewrite past pay.

A coach missed the 30-minute window with a mistake inside. Now what?

An admin fixes it in Coach Pay: open the session's Detail, override attendance or duration with a note. Overrides recompute from the original check-in rates.

A coach forgot to check in entirely.

The slot appears in Coach Pay's "Unlogged sessions" panel for 60 days. An admin logs it on the coach's behalf with the same attendance flow and the coach's own rates.

Can a coach see another coach's sessions or pay?

No. Coach logins are scoped to their own data, and the Coach Pay section is never granted to coaches. See permissions.

Can the correction window be longer than 30 minutes?

Yes, it is a setting: Coach Pay → Payout settings → correction window.

Coach Pay #

The whole payout machine: rate profiles per coach, every checked-in session priced, a twice-monthly payout email, and the tools to correct honest mistakes. Owner (and Sahand) only; coaches never see this page.

How a coach's pay is computed

Regular sessionBase rate + (athletes attended × per-athlete bonus), capped. A 1-athlete session pays base only. Lucas's reference profile: $35 base, +$5 per athlete, $80 cap. So 1 athlete $35/hr, 2 → $45, 4 → $55, 8 → $75, 10+ caps at $80.
Group bonus offFlat base regardless of headcount: the Foundations class model.
CampFlat camp rate (bonus only if that coach's camp-bonus toggle is on).
Shadow timeFlat shadow rate for paid standby, logged by the coach in quarter-hours. Lucas: $25/hr.
Pay per sessionHourly rate × session length. A 90-minute session at $55/hr pays $82.50.

Attendance is the input, not bookings. Rates are snapshotted at check-in, so changing a coach's rates later never rewrites history: changes apply to future check-ins only.

The payout email

  • Fires automatically on the 15th (covering the 1st to the 15th) and the 30th or last day of the month (covering the 16th to month-end), at about 8:30 in the morning Pacific.
  • Goes to the payout recipient (default dre@afsoccer.club) with an optional CC (Sahand's address, once set). One table per coach grouped by session type and rate, plus a grand total. Andre pays manually from it; the system never moves money.
  • Late additions: a session checked in after its period's email already went out is not lost. It rides the next email in its own "late additions" section. Nothing double-reports, nothing disappears.
  • Buttons on this page: Send test to me (a preview to your own inbox, does not mark anything as reported), Send now for real (guarded, marks sessions reported), and Print for a PDF copy.

Fixing things

  • Flagged sessions (zero attendees) wait in the sessions table for an admin decision.
  • Override: open a session's Detail to correct attendance or duration with a note. Pay recomputes from the check-in-time rates.
  • Void: kills a bad log and frees the slot to be re-logged. Blocked once the session has been payout-emailed, to keep the books honest.
  • Payout settings: recipient, CC, and the coach correction window (default 30 minutes).

Why is a coach's session missing from the payout preview?

Either it was never checked in (look in Unlogged sessions), it is flagged at $0 awaiting review, or it was checked in after the email went out and will ride the next one as a late addition.

We changed Lucas's rates mid-period. What happens?

Sessions already checked in keep their snapshot rates; new check-ins use the new rates. The payout email shows them as separate honest rows.
